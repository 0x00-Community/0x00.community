Welcome to the new home of the 0x00 community.

0x00 brings together open-source work on systems software — compilers, language runtimes, garbage collectors, GPU drivers and AI accelerator toolchains — under one vendor-neutral roof.

## What's here

- **[Projects](/projects/)** — every project in the [0x00-Community](https://github.com/0x00-Community) organization. The [j8 book](https://j8.app238.com/) has a site of its own.
- **[Docs](/docs/)** — how the community works and how to contribute.
- **Blog** — releases, engineering notes and community news, starting with this post.

Everything on this site is available in English and Simplified Chinese; use the language switch at the top right.

## Get involved

Read the [contributing guide](/docs/contributing/), pick a project, and open an issue or a pull request. We're glad you're here.
