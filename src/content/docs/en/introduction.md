0x00 is an open-source community for **systems software**: compilers, language runtimes, garbage collectors, GPU drivers and toolchains for AI accelerators. Every project is developed in public on [GitHub](https://github.com/0x00-Community) and released under an open-source license.

## Principles

- **Vendor-neutral.** Projects are general-purpose. Hardware or company-specific work lives behind clean interfaces, so anyone can build on it.
- **Built from first principles.** We prefer understanding a problem down to the metal over wrapping an existing black box.
- **Open by default.** Design discussions, roadmaps and reviews happen in the open.
