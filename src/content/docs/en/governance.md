> This model is a draft and will be refined together with the community.

## The main community

The main 0x00 community is not controlled by any single company. Sponsors support it but **do not get voting rights** by sponsoring. Decisions about the main community are made in the open by its committee and maintainers.

## Sub-communities

Organizations that want a closer working relationship can run a **sub-community** under 0x00 — for example around a particular chip or product line. Within its own sub-community, a sponsoring organization has a larger say in priorities and direction.

## Sponsorship

Support can go to the main community or to a specific sub-community. Downstream users never need to sponsor to use 0x00 software.

## What the community offers

- Real product scenarios to build against
- Incubation for new projects
- Recognition that spreads with the ecosystem
- Contributors who flow back into member projects
