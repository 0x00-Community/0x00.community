## Article 1. Definitions

1. **Community**: the 0x00 Community and the individuals and organizations that accept this Covenant and take part in 0x00 community projects ("community members").
2. **Project**: a project published under the 0x00 Community that declares this Covenant applies to it.
3. **Author**: the natural person who created the project. Each project names its author in its README or LICENSE.
4. **Community output**: everything produced in or for a project, including but not limited to code, documentation, designs, data, models, weights and other assets, whether produced by the author or by other contributors.
5. **Version**: a snapshot of the project explicitly designated by the author through a version number, tag or commit identifier.
6. **Supply**: the author making a version publicly available to the community, for example by publishing it in a community repository, tagging it, or releasing its artifacts.

## Article 2. Ownership

1. All intellectual property in community output, including copyright, patent rights and any other rights, belongs to the author personally.
2. By submitting a contribution to a project, a contributor agrees to assign all intellectual property in that contribution to the author, to the extent permitted by law.
3. For any right that cannot be assigned by law, the contributor grants the author an exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, sublicensable, worldwide license, and agrees not to assert that right against the author or the author's licensees.

## Article 3. Supplied versions: the community's perpetual right of use

1. Once the author supplies a version, community members hold a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free right to use that version.
2. The right of use covers running, copying, studying and modifying that version, and redistributing it and its modifications within the community under this Covenant.
3. The author also grants community members a license under any patents necessary to practice that version, with the same scope as paragraphs 1 and 2.
4. The rights in this Article are not withdrawn or narrowed by any later decision of the author.

## Article 4. Later versions

1. The author alone decides whether to supply any later version.
2. The community holds no right of use, or any other right, in a version the author has not supplied.
3. The community's rights in supplied versions do not extend to any version that has not been supplied.

## Article 5. Permissions

All rights not expressly granted by this Covenant are reserved by the author. Any permission beyond this Covenant may only be granted by the author in person and in writing. No other person, organization, contributor or platform may grant permission on the author's behalf.

## Article 6. Disclaimer

Projects are provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, express or implied. In no event shall the author or the contributors be liable for any claim, damages or other liability arising from a project.

## Article 7. Versions and effect

1. This is version 1.0 of the Covenant. Projects may cite the fixed address `/covenant/LICENSE4project/1.0/`.
2. Later versions of this Covenant do not affect the rights in versions supplied under this version.
